## Splash screen (ui.activities.SplashActivity + core.splash + drawable-nodpi/splash_bg_*)
- **The splash is a 20-second key-art show, and the show is the clock.** `SplashActivity` mounts `CaramelSplashScreen` and hands over when `SplashShow.finishedAt` is true; the version warm-up (`VersionManager.initializeAsync`) runs *beside* the show and no longer gates it. Do not reintroduce a warm-up-driven navigation: a fast install used to flash the art for a moment and a slow one held a frozen screen.
- **All timing arithmetic lives in `SplashShow`** (`core/splash`), which is pure and Android-free, so `SplashShowTest` pins it without a device: `indexAt`, `incomingAt`, `fadeAt`, `progressAt`, `slideProgressAt`, `finishedAt`. The composable reads those answers and owns no durations of its own. `DEFAULT_TOTAL_MS` is 20_000, `DEFAULT_FADE_MS` 900, `DEFAULT_SLIDES` 5; `of(...)` refuses a fade that does not fit its slide.
- **Which art a launch shows is `SplashArt.pick`** — a Fisher-Yates draw of `DEFAULT_SLIDES` distinct catalogue indices, with `avoidFirst` skipping the slide the previous launch opened on (stored in the `splash_show` SharedPreferences, by *catalogue index*, not resource id). `SplashArtTest` pins distinctness and the no-repeat-opening rule.
- **The catalogue is `SplashArtCatalog`** — 18 `drawable-nodpi/splash_bg_NN.jpg`, 1280x720, re-encoded from the Minecraft key-art wallpapers on `minecraft.net/en-us/collectibles`, The Garden Awakens onward. They are `nodpi` because they are photographic and scale by aspect, not by density. Adding a picture is one entry in the array plus the file; the splash picks from whatever length the array has.
- **The hand-over is one motion.** The composable fades itself out over the show's last 420ms (`exitAlpha`), `SplashActivity` plays `res/raw/splash_pop.wav` through `SplashSound` (a `SoundPool`, best-effort — audio failure is logged once and the splash continues silently) and calls `overridePendingTransition(R.anim.splash_handover_in, R.anim.splash_handover_out)`.
- **A backstop navigates anyway** after the show's length plus 1.5s (`MainThread.Scope`), because the splash is the only `LAUNCHER` entry: if its clock never reports, the app must still leave the screen.
- The art is bundled, so the show needs no network and no disk cache. `CaramelSplashHost.setContent(composeView, art, show, preparing, onFinished)` is the Java-facing seam — a `@Composable` cannot be called from Java.
